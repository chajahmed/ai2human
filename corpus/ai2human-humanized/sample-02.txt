Patient care and continuous monitoring have a new frontier, and AI is pushing the healthcare system past the hospital walls using the Internet of Medical Things, or IoMT for short. Physiological data gets grabbed nonstop by wearables, smartwatches, and implantable sensors, heart rate variability, blood glucose levels, electrocardiogram readings, all of it. That constant stream of data is processed in real time by AI algorithms, which then flag anything off for patients and providers to act on fast. Take smartwatches with AI-driven ECG, for instance, catching atrial fibrillation in people without symptoms; that condition is a big stroke risk. So this passive ongoing monitoring shifts things toward preventative care. Early interventions happen. Fewer emergency room visits and hospital readmissions follow. Chronic conditions, diabetes, heart failure, benefit the most here.
