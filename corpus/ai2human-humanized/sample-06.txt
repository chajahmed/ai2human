Robots fit these situations naturally. Breathing is not something they do. So the air could be completely unbreathable, full of poison, and the machine still operates just fine. They can also be engineered to take temperatures way beyond what any fire suit was ever designed to handle. On top of that, they carry sensors that pick out specific chemical compounds in real time. A commander on the safe side of the line can see what they are dealing with, without ever sending a person into that hot zone. And if something blows up, a robot that gets destroyed is just a piece of equipment that needs replacing. When a firefighter gets destroyed, that is a very different kind of loss.
