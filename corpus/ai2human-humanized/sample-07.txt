A number of militaries have put serious money into firefighting robots for use on naval vessels. Ship fires present a unique set of challenges. Water is everywhere, yet in many compartments it's the absolute worst thing to use, confined spaces and complex structural layouts come into play as well. The US Navy has built and tested humanoid robots that can handle firefighting hoses aboard ships, navigating corridors and climbing ladders just like a human crew member would. Why the humanoid shape matters is that ships are designed around how people move, so a robot matching that design can use the existing layout and gear without needing any changes.
