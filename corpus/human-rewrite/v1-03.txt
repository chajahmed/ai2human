In terms of medicine, it has been a "one size fits all" until now.So far, most of us can remember, medicines have been a "one size fits all" situation. If you have a certain form of cancer, you're treated with a standard course of chemotherapy or radiation. However, humans are not all created equal and what is effective for one might have no effect on the next. That's where the magic of precision medicine kicks in, greatly aided by AI. There are billions of pieces of information in our DNA and if we were to try and work out through manual methods what mutation is causing a particular disease it would feel like looking for a needle in a haystack. AI loves haystacks. It can analyze a patient's DNA and match it with large databases to inform an oncologist of which targeted treatment will most likely be effective. It eliminates the need to speculate about treatment and saves patients from suffering from harsh side effects from medications that were never likely to do them any good.
