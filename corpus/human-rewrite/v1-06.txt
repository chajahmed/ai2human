For autonomous cars, this requires a combination of advanced technology in the hardware and intelligent software to be able to safely operate without the constant input of humans. Several sensors gather environmental information and feed it to artificial intelligence algorithms that make real-time driving decisions. Cameras deliver visual data, which include identifying road lanes, traffic lights, pedestrians and cyclists, and other vehicles. The radar sensor is used to determine the speed and distance of surrounding objects, enabling the vehicle to detect moving objects even in bad weather. LiDAR systems use laser beams to accurately measure the distance to surrounding objects, creating very detailed and accurate 3-D maps of the environment.
