Global Positioning System (GPS) technology combines with high-definition digital maps to accurately locate the vehicle and to find the best possible route. Sensor fusion techniques include all of the information gathered by the sensors, forming a complete picture of the surroundings. Every time cars drive a few kilometers, they accumulate more data that machine learning models use to further enhance their performance. After the environmental analysis, the autonomous driving software calculates the steering angle, the acceleration and the braking force, and the position in the lane while keeping safe distances from other vehicles. The calculations happen on a regular basis within a fraction of a second, enabling the vehicle to act swiftly to different road conditions.
