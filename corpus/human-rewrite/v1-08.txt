Self-driving cars or driverless cars are one of the most notable technological developments in today's transportation system. The vehicles use a combination of artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning, computer vision, sensors, cameras, radar, LiDAR, and advanced software systems to operate with little or no human involvement. The main goal of AVs is to make the roads safer, improve the efficiency of transportation, alleviate traffic congestion and increase mobility for all who cannot drive themselves, including elderly citizens and people with disabilities. With the increasing urbanization and the problems associated with it, as well as the ever growing level of traffic, autonomous driving technology has become a potential game-changer for the future of traffic. Bills of dollars are being spent by governments, carmakers and tech firms on research and development to create commercially viable, reliable and safer self-driving cars. Autonomous cars are continuously being developed, but they have already shown impressive abilities in controlled environments and on certain public roads. The fact that they are widely adopted can transform mobility, change urban infrastructure and make the city more connected and intelligent.
