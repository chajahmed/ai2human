The primary target of the 2026 threat landscape is identity—AI is the power. With the advent of autonomous AI agents as first-class citizens in enterprise systems, the traditional network perimeter is further eroding over the years. Gartner's 2026 trends highlight the challenges of identity and access management (IAM) caused by the new reality of AI agents, including challenges related to identity registration, governance, and credential authorisation for non-human actors. Much like a human employee, an AI agent that can act on behalf of a company must have an identity, permissions and an audit trail – but most identity governance frameworks weren't built to support non-human, self-directing actors.
