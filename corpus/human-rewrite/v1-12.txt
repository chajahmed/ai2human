Xie et al. (2025) showed an AIoT-enabled building digital twin for a super real-time fire forecasting system, which is defined as fire prediction being ahead of the spread of the fire (F). The system is developed using neural networks, which enable a building model to be synchronised in space and fed with continuous IoT sensor data. The authors note that EV battery fires in confined spaces is an application that they have not considered, creating a clear linkage between their contribution to the architecture and the need to fill the void that their proposed tool addresses. Kim et al. (2024) demonstrated the ability to proactively stage resources at ignition events (F), an operational capability directly applicable to the proposed resource dashboard for the emergency responder.
