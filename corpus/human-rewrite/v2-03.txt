The most prominent application of robotics in medicine is robotic assisted surgery. Companies like the da Vinci Surgical System allow surgeons to be more in control when performing complex procedures than they are with traditional methods. The robotic arm is able to spin 360 degrees as well as damp out the tremors of an operator's hands, which means it is able to perform delicate tasks with greater precision than ever before. These have been used to great effect in various urological, cardiac, gynecological, and other surgical procedures. Numerous studies have demonstrated that robotic surgery has less blood loss, shorter hospital stays, quicker recoveries, and fewer complications than traditional open surgery.
