Rehabilitation robotics has become an important aid in the recovery of patients with stroke, spinal cord injury and other neurological diseases. Robotic limbs and exoskeleton devices help patients regain mobility and strength in their limbs through controlled and repetitive movement. They offer steady therapy sessions that a human therapist could not provide them on a regular basis. What's more, they have gained objective data collection which allows healthcare providers to monitor the progress quantitatively and tweak the treatment plan after all.
