Robotic systems are being used more and more in diagnostic techniques, especially in radiology and pathology. Automated ultrasound robots can conduct standardized scanning under consistent pressure and positions which can lower the pressure variation among operators. In the medical field, robots help in the preparation and examination of tissue samples, enhancing accuracy and efficiency. In addition, AI-driven robotic systems are being created to analyze medical images, which could detect any abnormalities that may not be detected by human observers.
