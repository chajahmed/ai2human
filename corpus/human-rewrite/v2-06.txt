Service robots are starting to assume supportive role in patient care settings. These robots can move medicines, linens and equipment around hospitals, relieving nurses of their time. Some sophisticated systems can track patients' vital signs, remind them to take their medicine and even offer limited companionship to the elderly or the lonely. Telepresence robots came to the fore during the COVID-19 pandemic as a way to connect with patients who are infected, thereby reducing the risk of exposure in a healthcare setting.
