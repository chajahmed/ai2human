Supercomputers play a vital role in scientific research. Supercomputers are used in areas like physics, chemistry and astronomy to simulate phenomena in the real world with a very high degree of accuracy. One area of climate science is the use of supercomputers to simulate the climate of both the earth's atmosphere and oceans, which aids in understanding climate change patterns and understanding the effects of human activities on the environment. Supercomputers are important for analyzing huge amounts of satellite, sensor and historical data that is needed to understand complex systems, which are the subject of these simulations. But if it was not for these calculations, long-term climate forecast would be almost impossible to realize accurately.
