Supercomputers are having an impact in medicine and medical-healthcare, where they revolutionize research and development of treatments. They play a key role in dissecting genetic information, modeling molecular interactions, and speeding up the process of drug discovery. A supercomputer can simulate the spread of diseases and find potential cures in a matter of hours, versus the time of weeks or months taken by standard means, during global health emergencies like pandemics. They have, for example, been employed in research on protein structures, and in the discovery of compounds that may inhibit viruses. This has the potential to save countless lives by allowing quicker response to health threats, and also to speed up the research process.
