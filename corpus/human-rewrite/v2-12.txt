The traditional fire alarm system is based on simple threshold sensor detection and alarm triggering, where the alarm is activated when smoke concentration and temperature exceed a specified threshold. However, advanced sensor fusion and pattern recognition within AI-powered systems detect fires earlier. AI algorithms can analyse the unique variations that happen just before the onset of a fire, before flames appear, using data from a range of different sensors, such as smoke detectors, thermal cameras, gas sensors and even air quality monitors. Machine learning models based on large amounts of data regarding fire and non-fire events can help to differentiate between the real and false fires, which are common in traditional systems.
