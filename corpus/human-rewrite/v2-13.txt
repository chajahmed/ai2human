One of the major problems with traditional fire detection systems is false alarms, which cost millions of dollars per year in lost productivity and are often the reason for 'alarm fatigue' and missed alarms in the event of an actual fire. AI can solve this issue by using advanced classification algorithms to process several data points at once. These systems can distinguish between a cooking fire, a shower, and a real fire. As more data enters into the system, the algorithms keep learning and adjusting to the particular environment they're operating in, becoming more effective at identifying harmless behavior vs. dangerous behavior over time.
