Apart from real-time detection, AI also provides predictive features that are not possible with traditional systems. AI can learn from past incidents and workplace layouts, occupancies and fire conditions to suggest preventative actions where there may be potential increased fire risk. If, for instance, sensor readings indicate poor electrical conditions, or abnormal heat readings on particular equipment, the system can sound an alarm to warn facility personnel before a fire can even start. This change from a reactive to a proactive approach to fire safety is a key paradigm change in protection strategy.AI-powered systems are able to direct a more effective emergency response if a fire is detected. AI systems can be integrated into building management systems, so that they can automatically operate elevators, unlock exits, modify ventilation systems to slow the spread of fire and direct occupants along the most efficient path for evacuation. Computer vision systems can track the actual situation and detect the position, extent, number of occupants in the building, and available exits. This information is shared with building users and emergency responders, helping to streamline and accelerate response.
