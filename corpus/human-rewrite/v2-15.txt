Mass communication is the dissemination of information, messages, and content to large, diverse audiences, often geographically dispersed, using a variety of channels and technologies. Mass communication is different from interpersonal communication, which is communication between two or more people. It is an essential force in public opinion, a force that affects cultural values, helps spread news and generates social change. Mass communication has undergone a rapid transformation over the last 200 years that has seen it become one of the most potent influencers in modern society, from newspapers, radio and television to the internet and social media.
