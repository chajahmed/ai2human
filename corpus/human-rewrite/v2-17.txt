The essence of mass communication has undergone enormous change in today's digital era. The internet and social media such as Facebook, Instagram, Twitter (X), YouTube and TikTok have helped to remove the power from the traditional media gatekeepers and put it in the hands of everyday people. Citizens can now do with user-generated content what professionals do with “traditional” journalism. This democratization of communication has accelerated the speed at which information travels and it has increased the global connectivity. Within minutes, a single video or post can reach millions of people, and may lead to viral phenomena that can impact an election, inspire a social movement, or ruin a reputation. This is, however, a very fast pace with significant questions of accuracy and accountability.
