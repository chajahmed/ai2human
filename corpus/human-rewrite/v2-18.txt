Mechatronics is an engineering subject, which integrates mechanical, electrical and computer science and control systems with a focus on developing intelligent, automated systems and products. The name first appeared in Japan in the 60's and it has since become one of the most active and exciting fields in contemporary engineering. Mechatronics aims to develop systems that are smarter, efficient and more capable than systems designed by any individual engineering discipline. Mechatronic systems are ubiquitous in everyday life, such as in washing machines, digital cameras, industrial robots, self-driving cars, and medical devices. A mechatronic engineer needs to have a wide range of knowledge and expertise across mechanical, electronic, microsensor, microactuator, and microcontroller design, and software algorithms that can create the desired behaviors. This field is key to the development of automation and robotics, technologies transforming sectors like manufacturing, healthcare, agriculture, and aerospace. With the ongoing development of technology, mechatronics is playing a pivotal role in the design of artificial intelligence embedded systems, smart devices and the Internet of Things. It's the future of engineering innovation where collaborative thinking and working across disciplines are paramount to the solution of complex real world problems and the advancement of technologies.
