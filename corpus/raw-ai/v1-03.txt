For as long as most of us can remember, medicine has largely been a "one-size-fits-all" kind of deal. If you have a certain type of cancer, you get a standard round of chemotherapy or radiation. But human bodies aren't identical, and what works wonders for one person might do absolutely nothing for the next. This is where the magic of precision medicine comes in, powered heavily by AI. Our DNA contains billions of pieces of information, and trying to manually figure out which genetic mutation is causing a specific disease is like looking for a needle in a haystack. AI loves haystacks. It can scan a patient’s genetic code, compare it against massive databases, and tell an oncologist exactly which targeted therapy has the best chance of working. It takes the guesswork out of treatment and spares patients from enduring brutal side effects from drugs that were never going to help them anyway.
