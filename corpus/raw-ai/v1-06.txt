Autonomous cars rely on a combination of sophisticated hardware and intelligent software to navigate safely without constant human control. Multiple sensors continuously collect environmental data, which is processed by artificial intelligence algorithms to make driving decisions in real time. Cameras provide visual information by identifying road lanes, traffic lights, pedestrians, cyclists, and other vehicles. Radar sensors measure the speed and distance of surrounding objects, allowing the vehicle to detect moving obstacles even under poor weather conditions. LiDAR systems generate highly detailed three-dimensional maps of the environment by using laser beams to determine the position of nearby objects with exceptional accuracy.
