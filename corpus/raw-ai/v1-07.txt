Global Positioning System (GPS) technology works together with high-definition digital maps to determine the precise location of the vehicle and identify the most efficient route. Artificial intelligence combines all incoming sensor information through sensor fusion techniques, creating a comprehensive understanding of the surrounding environment. Machine learning models continuously improve vehicle performance by learning from millions of kilometers of driving data. Once environmental analysis is complete, the autonomous driving software determines steering angle, acceleration, braking force, and lane positioning while maintaining safe distances from other vehicles. These calculations occur continuously within fractions of a second, allowing the vehicle to respond rapidly to changing road conditions.
