Autonomous cars, commonly referred to as self-driving cars or driverless vehicles, represent one of the most significant technological advancements in modern transportation. These vehicles are designed to operate with minimal or no human intervention by utilizing a combination of artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning, computer vision, sensors, cameras, radar, LiDAR, and advanced software systems. The primary objective of autonomous vehicles is to improve road safety, enhance transportation efficiency, reduce traffic congestion, and provide greater mobility for individuals who are unable to drive, such as the elderly and people with disabilities. As urban populations continue to grow and traffic-related challenges increase worldwide, autonomous driving technology has emerged as a promising solution to transform the future of transportation. Governments, automobile manufacturers, and technology companies are investing billions of dollars into research and development to make self-driving vehicles safer, more reliable, and commercially viable. Although autonomous cars are still under continuous development, they have already demonstrated remarkable capabilities in controlled environments and selected public roads.
