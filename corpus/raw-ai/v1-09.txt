If AI is the engine of the 2026 threat landscape, identity has become its primary target. Traditional network perimeters have been dissolving for years under cloud adoption, but the arrival of autonomous AI agents as first-class actors within enterprise systems has accelerated this further. Gartner's 2026 trends note that the rise of AI agents introduces new challenges for identity and access management, particularly around identity registration, governance, and credential authorization for non-human actors. An AI agent that can act autonomously on a company's behalf requires an identity, permissions, and an audit trail in the same way a human employee does, yet most identity governance frameworks were never designed with non-human, self-directing actors in mind.
