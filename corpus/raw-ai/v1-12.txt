Xie et al. (2025) demonstrated an AIoT-powered building digital twin for what the authors term super real-time fire forecasting — prediction of fire spread faster than the spread itself occurs (F). The system integrates continuous IoT sensor data through neural networks within a spatially synchronised building model. The authors identify EV battery fires in confined spaces as an application they have not addressed, establishing a direct connection between their architectural contribution and the gap the proposed tool seeks to fill. Kim et al. (2024) showed that continuous sensor streams combined with digital twin analytics enable proactive resource staging before ignition events (F) — an operational capability directly relevant to the proposed tool’s emergency-responder dashboard.
