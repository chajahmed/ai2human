Robotic-assisted surgery remains the most prominent application of robotics in medicine. Systems like the da Vinci Surgical System enable surgeons to perform complex procedures with greater control than traditional methods. The robotic arms can rotate 360 degrees and filter out hand tremors, allowing for unprecedented precision in delicate operations. These systems have been successfully employed in urological surgeries, cardiac procedures, gynecological operations, and general surgical interventions. Studies have consistently shown that robotic surgery results in reduced blood loss, shorter hospital stays, faster recovery times, and fewer complications compared to conventional open surgery.
