In medicine and healthcare, supercomputers have revolutionized research and treatment development. They are instrumental in analyzing genetic data, simulating molecular interactions, and accelerating drug discovery. During global health crises, such as pandemics, supercomputers can model the spread of diseases and identify potential treatments in a fraction of the time required by traditional methods. For instance, they have been used to study protein structures and identify compounds that could inhibit viruses. This capability not only speeds up research but also has the potential to save countless lives by enabling faster responses to emerging health threats.
