Traditional fire alarm systems rely on simple threshold-based detection, triggering alarms when smoke concentration or temperature crosses a fixed level. AI-powered systems, however, employ advanced sensor fusion and pattern recognition to identify fire signatures much earlier. By combining data from multiple sensors—including smoke detectors, thermal cameras, gas sensors, and even air quality monitors—AI algorithms can detect the subtle changes that occur during the incipient stage of a fire, long before flames become visible. Machine learning models trained on vast datasets of fire and non-fire scenarios can distinguish between genuine threats and benign events, dramatically reducing the incidence of false alarms that plague conventional systems.
