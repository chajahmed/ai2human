Beyond real-time detection, AI enables predictive capabilities that traditional systems simply cannot offer. By analyzing historical data, building layouts, occupancy patterns, and environmental conditions, AI systems can identify areas of elevated fire risk and recommend preemptive measures. For example, if sensors detect deteriorating electrical conditions or unusual heat patterns in specific equipment, the system can alert facility managers before a fire even begins. This shift from reactive to proactive fire safety represents a fundamental evolution in protection strategy.When a fire is detected, AI-enhanced systems coordinate a more effective emergency response. Integration with building management systems allows AI to automatically control elevators, unlock exits, adjust ventilation systems to slow fire spread, and guide occupants along optimal evacuation routes. Computer vision systems can monitor real-time conditions, identifying the location and spread of fire, the number of people in the building, and which exits remain viable. This information is communicated to both building occupants and emergency responders, enabling faster, more coordinated action.
