Mass communication refers to the process of delivering information, messages, and content to large, diverse, and often geographically dispersed audiences through various channels and technologies. Unlike interpersonal communication, which occurs between individuals, mass communication is designed to reach thousands or millions simultaneously. It plays a central role in shaping public opinion, influencing cultural values, spreading news, and driving social change. From newspapers and radio to television, the internet, and social media platforms, mass communication has evolved dramatically over the past two centuries, becoming one of the most powerful forces in contemporary society.
