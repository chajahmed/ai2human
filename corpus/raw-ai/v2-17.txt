In today’s digital age, mass communication has undergone a profound transformation. The rise of the internet and social media platforms like Facebook, Instagram, Twitter (now X), YouTube, and TikTok has shifted power from traditional media gatekeepers to ordinary individuals. User-generated content now competes with professional journalism. This democratization of communication has accelerated the speed at which information travels and expanded global connectivity. A single video or post can reach millions within minutes, creating viral phenomena that can influence elections, spark social movements, or damage reputations overnight. However, this speed also comes with significant challenges regarding accuracy and accountability.
