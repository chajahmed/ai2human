Thermal runaway is the primary safety failure mode for lithium-ion batteries in EV applications, and its detection constitutes the foundational input signal required by any dynamic fire risk system positioning this theme within the battery-level hazard layer of the three-layer conceptual framework introduced in Section 1. The thirteen studies in this section can be roughly categorized into four technical sub-sections: Data-driven State Estimation and Fault Diagnosis, Multi-signal Fusion for Early Warning, Recurrent and Temporal Deep Learning for Sequence Prediction, Physics-informed, Graph-based and Interpretable Approaches. One difficulty in the four sub-areas is that there is no standardization of benchmark datasets (Zhang et al., 2023) and the gap exists between the well tested architectures in the laboratory conditions and architecture performance in deployed datasets reported in a variety of studies. This portion of the benchmarking is not only a methodological cumbersome but also a lack of confidence in being able to recommend an architecture that can be applied across battery chemistries and vehicle platforms.
